YogAI continuous-servo bench test

Standalone page for testing the YOGAI-CR firmware and calibrating the servos. It does not open the camera or touch YogAI.

Before you start: upload servo_tracker_continuous.ino with the Arduino IDE. Take the servo horns or the phone off the stand, clear everything from the servos, and keep your hand near the big STOP ALL button. This page cannot detect a stall or an end stop.

Pulses reported by the Arduino: yaw – µs · tilt – µs

2. Neutral calibration (one servo at a time)

With the servos armed and stopped, nudge the neutral until the servo stops creeping. Change by 1 µs steps near the end. The neutral is applied immediately.

3. Move test (hold a button)

"+" means a pulse longer than neutral. Note which way each servo turns; that decides the direction settings later. The stand moves only while the button is held.

Estimated rotation from centre (approximate, drifts): yaw 0° ± 0° · tilt 0° ± 0°

Restored from the last session and not verified. Move the stand to its centre by eye and press "Set centre here" if it was moved.

4. Speed range (per servo, per direction)

Minimum offset = the smallest pulse change from neutral that makes the servo turn reliably. Maximum offset = where it stops getting faster. Offsets are in µs.

The revolution times are only used for the approximate rotation estimate. Time one turn with the stand unloaded.

5. Safety parameters

Watchdog test: while armed, the page stops sending for 1.5 s. The Arduino must stop the servos and report the watchdog. Re-arm afterwards.
